Selection rules

  • Size: 11 problems, the historical average (10–15 per year).
  • Per contest, each rating band and category count is the 2016–2025 average share (2023 excluded), rounded to 11 slots.
  • Exactly one problem that no GNY team solved; past contests averaged about one.
  • At most two problems from any one year; no near-duplicates (the two Collatz and two unicycle problems).
  • Excluded: 2021 I (same as 2023 E); 2021 H and 2022 H (statement and judges' code disagree).
  • Every write-up was checked against the judges' code (high confidence).

Judging: four problems are on Kattis and can be submitted there. For the rest, compare your output against the judges' secret data linked on each problem (diff the files; problems with real-number output accept small differences). Ratings are estimates on the Codeforces scale; problems, statements and data are from the ICPC Greater New York Regional archive at acmgnyr.org (CC BY-NC-SA 3.0).
